Governing Equations (Bimodal Exhaust)

Critical Assumption: The plasma velocity equation assumes fragment kinetic energy is thermally coupled to the entire ablated mass ratio K (requiring a Marshak wave to propagate past the 10-20μm Bragg peak into millimeters of carbon). The efficiency of this energy recovery (η_energy) is the primary unvalidated variable.

1. Dual-Stream Effective Specific Impulse
v_eff = (v_ff + K * v_p) / (1 + K)
2. Plasma Velocity & Energy Recovery
v_p = √[ 2 * (η_energy * Q_ff / K - H_sub) ]

Where Q_ff = 3.595×10¹³ J/kg (Fragment KE), H_sub = 6.0×10⁷ J/kg (Carbon Sublimation).
