Where the released energy goes
—
Only the nine final fates partition the released power; they sum to 100% exactly. The first group splits that same power by origin, and the middle group is heat routed onto the block on its way to a final fate — both are views of energy counted once below, not extra energy. The residual re-forms the jet terms independently: the sublimation term from mass × enthalpy, and the jet kinetic term by taking the jet speed back through γ, so an error in the enthalpy split shows up instead of cancelling.
